Introduction

This software performs FRET-FLIM analysis using the phasor transformation of TTTR fluorescence decay data. Currently, only PicoQuant HydraHarp 2 T3 data is supported (.ptu).
Before FRET-FLIM data can be interpreted, the phasor plot has to be calibrated. To do that, load a measurement of a reference dye or a recording of the laser pulse. Choose a high image binning factor for the references (Loading Data -> Binning Factor), then adjust the left cutoff of the decay data (Histogram -> Cut-Off Left) so that the average lifetime matches the lifetime of the reference dye or a zero lifetime in case of a laser pulse measurement.
After calibration, the lifetime range has to be determined. Three measurements are required for that: donor-only, donor and acceptor coexpression, and donor-acceptor fusion construct. Load the respective experiments and go through the analysis procedure. The lifetime range is the basis for the interpretation of the biological experiments. The lifetimes of the donor-only (no FRET) and donor-acceptor fusion are set as range (Colorize -> Donor-only Lifetime / FRET-positive Control).
